YesOne — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YesOne ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the YesOne mobile app.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your email address, the photos you upload, clothing and closet details you enter, avatar and body information you provide, and content you post to social features (feed, chat, styling challenges). If you enable weather features we collect your approximate location, and when you subscribe we receive purchase and subscription details from Apple. We also collect basic usage and device information to operate and improve the app.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to organize your closet, generate outfit suggestions and virtual try-on results, run the features you choose to use, and operate social features. We never sell your personal data.

Photos, Face & Body (Try-On & Avatars)

To create avatars and virtual try-on images, you may upload photos that include your face and body. We use these photos only to generate the images you request, and we send them to AI image providers that process them on our behalf to return a result. We do not use facial-recognition technology and we do not create, capture, store, or use a scan of your face or body geometry, a "faceprint," or any other biometric identifier to identify you. We do not sell your photos and do not use them for advertising. Your uploaded photos and generated images are deleted when you delete the item or your account. These features are optional.

AI Processing & Third-Party Services

To provide AI features, we send some of your content to third-party AI providers that process it on our behalf. Specifically, clothing photos you upload may be sent to AI image services to detect, cut out, and enhance items and to generate try-on results, and text extracted from your emails (when you use email import) may be sent to OpenAI to identify clothing orders. This content is not anonymized before processing — it is sent as needed to perform the feature you requested. These providers process the data only to return a result to us and are not permitted to use it to identify you or for their own purposes.

Email Import (Optional)

If you choose to import clothing from your order emails, you connect your mailbox using an app-specific password. That password is stored only on your device (in the device keychain) and is never sent to our servers. Your emails are read on your device to find clothing orders; only the extracted order details (such as sender, subject, and a short snippet of order text and product images) are sent to our server and to OpenAI to identify items. We do not store the full contents of your emails. This feature is entirely optional and the app works fully without it. You can disconnect at any time.

Location (Optional)

If you enable weather-based outfit suggestions, the app requests your device location and sends your approximate coordinates to a third-party weather service (Open-Meteo) to fetch the local forecast. Your coordinates are used only to retrieve weather and are not stored on our servers or tied to your identity. This is optional and the app works fully without it.

Subscriptions & Payments

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status, which receives a purchase identifier and transaction details to keep your plan and credits in sync. We never receive or store your full payment card details.

Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure (Supabase) using industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from within the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account). Deletion removes your profile, photos, closet, posts, messages, and other account data from our systems, and signs you out. We may retain limited records where required by law or to prevent abuse. If you ask us to delete your data by email, we will do so within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

YesOne is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. We do not sell your personal information. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. To make a request, use in-app account deletion or email us below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
