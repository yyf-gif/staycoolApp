YesOne Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Overview

YesOne is a mobile application that helps users manage their wardrobe, try on outfits, and share looks with others. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

Information We Collect

  • Account information (email address) when you sign up
  • Clothing item photos you upload or scan
  • Wardrobe data you add to the app
  • Photos you provide to create your avatar and to generate virtual try-on images
  • Content you create in the app, including posts, outfits, and messages you send to other users
  • Purchase information when you buy a subscription or credits (processed by the App Store and our payments provider; we do not store your payment card details)
  • Usage and diagnostic data (such as which features you use and basic device information) to operate and improve the app

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core features: wardrobe management, outfit and try-on generation, ani>
  • To enable social features such as the feed, posts, and messaging between users
  • To process purchases and manage your subscription or credits
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the app

User-Generated Content, Community & Safety

YesOne includes social features where you can post content and message other users. You are responsible for the content you share. We do not tolerate objectionable content or abusive behavior.

  • You can report content or users you believe violate our guidelines
  • You can block other users
  • We review reports and may remove content or remove users who violate our guidelines

Email Import (Optional)

If you choose to import purchases from your inbox, YesOne asks you to create an email App Password — a separate, revocable password that is not your main account password. This App Password is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers. YesOne connects to your mailbox tnfirmation emails, and sends only the relevant text from those emails (sender, subject,and order details) to our servers to identify the clothing items you purchased.

  • We only process the text of order- and shipping-confirmation emails; we do not read your unrelated emails
  • We never store your email password
  • You can disconnect an account, or delete the App Password in your account settings, at any time
